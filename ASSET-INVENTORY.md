## What this means

None of this photography is cleared for public use. The studio press shots are the studios' own,
the lifestyle photographs are third-party, and the two typefaces that ship as files
(`caveat.woff`, with its OFL licence beside it) are the only assets here with an explicit licence.
This has to be settled before the site is shown outside a private review — alongside the studio
brand names, which appear throughout with invented class schedules under them.
