# VIRI — Privacy Policy

## Who we are

VIRI (Vitality Ritual) is a service for finding people to work out with. It is
run by Margaret Cole, in Washington, DC. You can reach us about anything in this
policy at **info@vitalityritual.org**.

VIRI is not a company. It is run by one person, and that person is responsible
for your data. If that changes, this page will say so.

## What this covers

This policy covers vitalityritual.org and the VIRI account you create on it.

## What we collect

**When you make an account:** your email address and a password. The password is
stored hashed — we never see it and cannot recover it for you.

**Your profile.** Some of this is required, most is not.

| | |
|---|---|
| Required | your name, the neighbourhood and city where you train |
| Required | your age &mdash; VIRI is for over-18s. Showing it on your profile is opt-in and off by default |
| Optional | a photograph, a short bio, your college and year, your industry |
| Matching | the activities you do and the times of week you usually train |

**What you do on VIRI:** sessions you log (activity, place, how long, how far,
any note or photograph, the date, who you were with), classes you add to your
plan, studios you save, people you connect with, and messages you send.

**Safety records:** if you report someone, we keep what you told us. If you
block someone, we keep that too.

**If you subscribe to the VIRI edit** we keep your email address, and nothing
else, so we can send it to you. You do not need an account to subscribe, and
subscribing does not make one. Every letter has an unsubscribe link.

We do **not** collect analytics. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising
network, and no third-party script on the site other than the one that connects
you to our database. Nobody is following you around the internet on our behalf.

## Why the optional fields matter

Your neighbourhood, your usual training times, the studios you save and the
sessions you log together describe where you are likely to be, and when. That is
the point of the product — it is how you find someone to train with — but it is
genuinely sensitive, more so than it first appears.

So: every one of those fields is optional, and you can remove any of them at any
time. Think about what you are comfortable with other members seeing before you
fill them in.

## Who else touches your data

We use four other companies to run VIRI. They process data on our behalf and are
not allowed to use it for anything else.

- **Supabase** — the database, your login, and your photographs
- **Resend** — sends your confirmation and password-reset emails
- **FormSubmit** — relays the contact form and report notifications to our inbox
- **GitHub Pages** — serves the website itself

The Explore map loads its tiles from **OpenStreetMap**. Links to studios and
articles take you to other companies' websites, which have their own policies.

**We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.** We would
disclose information if the law required it, or to protect someone from harm.

## Your photographs

Photographs are stored privately. They are not on a public address that can be
guessed or shared — each view is a link that expires. When a photograph is
uploaded, it is re-encoded, which strips embedded location data; a picture taken
at your gym does not carry your gym's coordinates into VIRI.

## What you can do

In **Settings**, at any time, without asking us:

- **Download my data** gives you one file containing everything VIRI holds about
  you, with your photographs inside it rather than as links that expire.
- **Delete my account** removes your profile, sessions, plans, saved studios,
  messages, connections and photographs. It is immediate and we cannot undo it.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights — to correct
information, to object to processing, or to complain to a regulator. Write to
info@vitalityritual.org and we will help.

## What we keep after you leave

When you delete your account, everything above goes immediately.

One exception: if somebody reported you, that report stays. Letting an account
deletion erase the record of harm done to another member would make the report
system worthless. Reports *you* filed about other people are deleted with your
account.

## Age

VIRI is for adults. You must be 18 or over to make an account, and we do not
knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you
believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

## Security

Your password is hashed. Photographs are private and reachable only by expiring
links. Access to every table is controlled at the database level, so one
member's account cannot read another's data even if something goes wrong in the
app. No system is perfect, and we will tell you promptly if something happens
that affects you.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you rather than
quietly updating the page.

## Notes for the lawyer — not for publication

1. **No entity, by decision.** Margaret has chosen to hold off on forming an
   LLC. So liability is personal and unlimited, the named controller is an
   individual, and there is no company standing between her and a claim. This
   raises the stakes on the Terms of Service waiver (item 7) rather than
   lowering them — that disclaimer is now the only thing doing that job.
2. **Sensitive inference.** Neighbourhood + training times + saved studios +
   logged sessions is effectively a location-and-schedule pattern for
   individually identified women. Worth advice on whether any of this is
   "precise geolocation" under state privacy statutes, and on the standard of
   care expected.
3. **Jurisdiction.** DC-based, expecting US users initially. Need advice on CCPA
   thresholds, the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar state laws given
   fitness data, and whether any EU users trigger GDPR obligations including a
   lawful basis analysis and a representative.
4. **Age.** Now 18, required rather than optional, and enforced by the only
   selectable birth years. Display on the profile is a separate opt-in.
5. **Retention of reports about deleted accounts.** Asserted above as legitimate
   interest. Needs confirming, and a defined retention period rather than
   indefinite.
6. **Processors.** No DPAs signed with Supabase, Resend or FormSubmit. FormSubmit
   is a free relay carrying contact-form content; it should probably be replaced
   with Resend before launch so there is one fewer processor and an actual
   agreement.
7. **Terms of Service** does not exist yet. Needs writing alongside this —
   particularly a disclaimer about members meeting in person, which is the
   central risk of the product.
8. **Breach notification.** Promised "promptly" above; should match the specific
   statutory deadlines that apply.
