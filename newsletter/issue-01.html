Socialize your fitness.

Hi, I’m Margaret.

Every week I see the same faces in my classes. The woman who always takes the bike by the window. The runner on my 6am loop. We’ve shared hours of sweat and never said more than “is this spot taken?”

That’s why I started VIRI: a place for women in their twenties figuring out how to train, work and have a life at the same time.

Every Sunday, The VIRI Edit brings you a first look at what’s new on VIRI, an older piece worth rereading, and one thing I’m trying myself. It will be short, and it will be honest.

See you next Sunday,
Margaret
