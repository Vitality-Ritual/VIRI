## The short version

VIRI helps you find people to exercise with. We do not check who those people
are. Meeting someone from VIRI is your decision and your risk, exactly as it
would be if you met them at the gym without us. Please read the section headed
**Meeting other members** — it is the most important part of this page.

mean her. "You" means you.

By making an account you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not
make an account.

## 2. You must be 18

VIRI is for adults. You must be 18 or over. We ask your age when you join, and
accounts we believe belong to someone under 18 are removed.

## 2a. Who can join

VIRI is for women. Trans women are women, and are welcome.

We do not verify this, and we are not going to — gender cannot be checked

## 3. Your account

Give us accurate information, and keep it accurate. Choose a password you do
not use elsewhere, and keep it to yourself. What happens on your account is
your responsibility.

One person, one account. Do not make an account for anybody else, and do not
pretend to be someone you are not.

## 4. What VIRI is, and what it is not

VIRI shows you other members whose activities and training times overlap with
yours, and lets you talk to them. That is all it is.

**We do not check who anybody is.** There is no identity verification, no
background check, no vetting, and no screening of any kind. We do not confirm
that a name is real, that a photograph is of the person using the account, or
that anything written on a profile is true. Anyone can make an account.

We are not a party to anything you arrange with another member. We do not
supervise meetings, we are not present at them, and we are not responsible for
what members do.

We also do not run the studios or classes listed on VIRI, and we are not
affiliated with them unless we say so. Check schedules, prices and requirements
with the studio itself.

## 5. Meeting other members

**This is the part that matters.**

VIRI exists so you can meet people in person. That carries real risk. The people
you meet are strangers, we have not checked them, and we cannot protect you from
them.

**You decide whether to meet someone, and you accept the risk of doing so.** You
are responsible for your own safety, and for deciding what information to share
and with whom.

Some things worth doing, every time:

- Meet in public, in a class or a studio, not somewhere private
- Tell someone you trust where you are going and who you are meeting
- Make your own way there and back
- Leave if you feel uncomfortable — you never owe anyone an explanation
- If you are in danger, contact the emergency services, not us

Think carefully before putting your neighbourhood, your regular training times
and the studios you attend on your profile. Together they describe where you can
be found, and when. Every one of those fields is optional.

You can **block** anyone, which stops them messaging you or sending you a
request, and **report** anyone to us.

We read every report. Reports about harassment, or about feeling unsafe with
someone, are read **every day**. Everything else we aim to act on within two
business days. We cannot undo harm that has already happened.

**If you are in danger, contact the emergency services.** We are not an
emergency service, we are not monitoring VIRI around the clock, and we cannot
respond like one.

Only post photographs you took or have the right to use, and do not post
pictures of other people without their agreement.

Members who do these things lose their accounts.
